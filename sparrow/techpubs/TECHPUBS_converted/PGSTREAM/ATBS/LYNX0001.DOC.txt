Atari Technical Bulletin
Number: LYNX-0001
Date: May 8, 1992
Page 1 of 1


Product(s) Affected:	LYNX
Model:				LYNX I
Type of ATB:			As Needed
ECO #: 				1418, January 21, 1992

Reason for ATB:

To correct premature failures of the internal power connector on the LYNX I.


Notes:

The power connector in the LYNX I needs to be replaced with the more reliable LYNX II power connector. Sub-assemblies affected are CA200552-001 REV A and CA200592-001, 002, 003, 004 REV K.


Symptoms of Problem:

Internal power connector fails to supply power to unit after prolonged use.


Solution:

1.	Replace power jack part number C101979-001 with power jack part number C101984-001.
