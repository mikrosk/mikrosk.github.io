1.0 GENERAL

This specification describes the detailed requirements for a 117 VAC 60 HZ version of a
switching power supply used in Atari computers.

Suppliers of this part must supply to the Atari Microsystems Product Engineering, Product Safety,
and Document Control Departments copies of all Engineering Change Orders on this product in
writing.

Vendor must supply an Electrical Schematic and Part List for this power supply.

Changes to this power supply affecting Regulatory Agency Approval status cannot be done
without the prior approval of  Atari Product Engineering.


2.0 INPUT ELECTRICAL REQUIREMENTS

2.1 AC INPUT VOLTAGE:           90 to 129 VAC (RMS) 117 VAC Nominal

2.2 AC INPUT FREQUENCY:         55 to 65 Hz

2.3 AC INPUT CURRENT:           0.9 Amperes (RMS) for 117 VAC operation

2.4 MAXIMUM INRUSH CURRENT:     35 Amperes for 117 VAC


3.0 OUTPUT ELECTRICAL REQUIREMENTS

          Output 1   Output 2
_________________________________________________________

3.1 Output Voltage:  +5 VDC     +12 VDC

3.2 Minimum Load Current:       1.5 Amp    0.0 Amp

3.3 Maximum Load Current:       7.0 Amp    0.2 Amp

3.4 Line Regulation: ±5%        ± 10%

3.5 Load Regulation: ± 5%       ± 10%

3.6 Cross Regulation:± 5%       ± 10%


3.7 Specification of cross regulation:

(1) 5 VDC, 1.5 Amp, 12 VDC, 0.18 Amp
(2) 5 VDC, 7.0 Amp, 12 VDC, 0.0 Amp

3.8 Ripple:                100 millivolts p-p

3.9 Noise:                 100 millivolts p-p

3.10 Peak Current:         +5 VDC peak current of 10 amperes for not more than 10 seconds
                           with +12 VDC output at full load.

3.11 +5 VDC Set Point:     The +5 VDC output must be set to +5.05 ±0.1 VDC with a +5
                           VDC output having a load of 4.0 amperes.


4.0 OVERALL PERFORMANCE

4.1 Total Output Power:         37.4 watts

4.2 Efficiency:            55% minimum at full load, minimum frequency.

4.3 Hold up time:               10 milliseconds at 100% load and nominal input voltage.

4.4 Switching frequency:        20 KHZ (minimum)

4.5 Temperature coefficient:    ± 0.04% per degree Centigrade

4.6 Power on delay time:        150 milliseconds (maximum)


5.0 FEATURES

5.1 Short circuit protection:   All outputs are to be equipped with short circuit protection.
                                If any of the outputs become overloaded, the power supply
                                should switch off within 20 milliseconds, all outputs should
                                not exceed their limit.

5.2 Overshoot:             (5% of the nominal output voltage) at turn on and off with
                           the outputs not exceeding the limit.

6.0 ENVIRONMENTAL

6.1 Operating Temperature: 0 to +50 degrees Centigrade

6.2 Operating Humidity:         20% to 90% Relative Humidity

6.3 Operating Altitude:         0 to 3000 meters

6.4 Storage Temperature:        -20 to +80 degrees Centigrade

6.5 Storage Humidity:           0% to 90% Relative Humidity

6.6 Storage Altitude:           0 to 15,000 meters

6.7 Cooling:                    Free air convection

6.8 Audible Noise:              Not applicable

6.9 Vibration Proof:       This specification and electrical characteristics of the
                           product can be maintained after being subjected to the
                           vibration with a frequency variation range of 10 Hz to 55
                           Hz at an amplitude of 1 millimeter, and cycle time of 1
                           minute for 2 hours in each of the X,Y and Z directions.

6.9 Impact:                     When packed for shipment, the product shall work normally
                                after a drop from a 100 centimeter height on each flat
                                surface of the shipping container.


7.0  BURN IN

7.1 Burn In Parameters:    24 hours at 45 degrees Centigrade (±5 degrees Centigrade) with
                           power on/off cycling at full load and 117 VAC nominal line
                           voltage.

7.2  Warranty:       One year warranty

8.3  Fuse:           UL, CSA Approval

VDE 0871 6.68 Class B


10.0 LEAKAGE CURRENT

10.1 Leakage current:      0.5 milliamperes maximum


11.0 DIELECTRIC WITHSTANDING VOLTAGE (HIPOT)

11.1 Input to Output:      1500 VAC 10 milliamperes 60 seconds


12.0 INSULATION RESISTANCE

12.1 Input to Output:      10 megohms minimum


13.0 RELIABILITY

13.1 Mean Time Between Failures:     50,000 hours minimum at 75% load at a +35 degree
                                     Centigrade ambient temperature.

13.2 On / Off Cycles:                The power supply shall withstand a minimum of
                                     5000 power on/off cycles without failure rate
                                     degradation.

16.0 SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS:

16.1 MAXIMUM COMPONENT HEIGHT:

ALL COMPONENTS ON THE PC BOARD OTHER THAN HEAT SINKS CANNOT EXCEED
THE LIMIT OF 31 MILLIMETERS IN HEIGHT.

16.2 INSULATION OF HEAT SINK:

HEIGHT OF HEAT SINKS MUST NOT EXCEED THE MAXIMUM HEIGHT OF 43.78 MM
SHOWN IN PARAGRAPH 17.1, POWER SUPPLY REFERENCE DRAWING. IF THE HEAT
SINK HEIGHT IS GREATER THAN 43.78 MM, THE HEAT SINK MUST BE INSULATED
ALONG ITS UPPER MOST EDGE. IN NO CASE CAN THE HEAT SINK HEIGHT EXCEED
THE 43.78 MM HEIGHT LIMITATION. THIS IS NEEDED TO PREVENT SHORTING OF
THE HEAT SINK TO A METAL SHIELD ABOVE THE POWER SUPPLY.

16.3 FRAME GROUND REFERENCE:

The power supply must provide the option to connect the +5 VDC Output Return and the +12
VDC Output Return to frame ground.

18.0 MARKINGS

The power supply must be marked with the manufacturer's logo, manufacturer's part number and
revision, the unit's serial number, and Atari part number.


19.0 QUALITY ASSURANCE PROVISIONS

The power supply must meet Atari's "Qualification, Reliability, Acceptance Specifications".


20.0 PACKAGE FOR SHIPMENT

All power supplies shipped to this specification shall be packed to prevent physical damage,
corrosion and deterioration during shipment.
