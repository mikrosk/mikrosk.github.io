SECTION FIVE:
SYMPTOM CHECKLIST


This section gives a brief summary of common problems and their most probable causes. For more detail, refer to the section of troubleshooting in this document, or the Diagnostic Cartridge Troubleshooting Guide.


5.1   DISPLAY PROBLEMS

Probable Cause

No power (check LED), bad COMBO IC, bad VIDEL IC. See Section 3.3: Troubleshooting a Dead Unit.

VIDEL IC, COMBO IC, SDMA IC, MC68030. Use diagnostic cartridge with terminal connected via RS232 port.

Bad SIMM modile, COMBO IC, VIDEL IC. Use the diagnostic cartridge.

Video cable or connector. SGC DAC, VIDEL IC. Check signals with oscilloscope.

Verify that the monochrome monitor detect bit is being 	grounded when monochrome monitor is plugged in. 	Check MFP GPIP bit 7; replace MFP.


COMBO IC. Use diagnostic cartridge.

Modulator, phase locked loop. Trace signal with oscilloscope.

White Screen


Dots/Bars on Screen


One Color Missing
