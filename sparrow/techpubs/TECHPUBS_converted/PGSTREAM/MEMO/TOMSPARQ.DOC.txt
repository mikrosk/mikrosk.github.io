To:		Tom Ulaszek

Subject:	FALCON030 Product Specification

Date:	July 13, 1992

cc		Ira Golstein

Well, here it is. Despite a problem with my air conditioning unit at home, which took all day Friday to repair, I have finished the preliminary release of the FALCON030 Product Specification. Please review it and pass it on to Jerry and Scott for additional comments. I have made several corrections based on the latest information received from John Hoenig and Tracy Hall. Sincerely
