Dallas Office Memo


To:		All Subsidiaries
From:	Marty Pennock
Subject:	ABC386SX Western Digital Service Information

Date:	February 28, 1992

cc		Ira Goldstein - ATX
		Tom Ulaszek - ATX
		Richard Miller - SUVL


Enclosed please find a set of servicing information compiled for the ABC386SX original build. The schematic enclosed is as good a release as could be found, but not completely up to date. Bill of Material lists should cover most of the parts needed, except for country specific TOS. Also included are chipset data sheets for the custom chipset used in the design. Standard chip data sheets,  such as the 80386SX, are not included as they may be found off the shelf. I hope that this will help in the troubleshooting and repair of these units.


Sincerely


Marty Pennock
