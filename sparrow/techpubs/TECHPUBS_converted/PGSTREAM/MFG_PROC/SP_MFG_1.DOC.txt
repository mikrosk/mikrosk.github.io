1.0	Overview

1.1	Purpose

This procedure provides the basis for a production standard   intended to control the processes required to manufacture Falcon 030 systems of consistent quality.

1.2	Scope

This procedure covers the documentation, production standards, quality requirements, and assembly processes necessary to manufacture Falcon 030 finished goods.

1.3	Applicable documents

The following documents including any subordinate documents — assembly drawings, sub assembly drawings, bills of material, fabrication drawings, component specification drawings, test procedures, and schematics — of the revision currently in effect form a part of this procedure, and may provide details not covered by this procedure. Therefore, the following documents take precedence over this procedure in resolving any differences.
