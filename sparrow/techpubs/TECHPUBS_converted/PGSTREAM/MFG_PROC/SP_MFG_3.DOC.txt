3.0	INCOMING INSPECTION

1.	Application
This procedure shall be applied for incoming inspection of all listed components.

2.	Purpose
To prevent the use of materials in the finished product which do not conform to the specifications.

3.	Inspection method

3.1	Type
Lot by lot acceptance sampling

3.2	Standard
MIL-STD-105D normal inspection with single sampling

3.3	Level
Inspection level II unless otherwise specified

4.	Defect classification
Critical defect - a defect likely to result in hazardous or unsafe conditions for individuals using maintaining, or storing the product.
Major defect - a defect, other than critical, that is likely to result in failure, or to reduce materially the usability of the product for its intended purpose.
Minor defect - a defect that is not likely to reduce materially the usability of the product for its intended purpose, or is a departure from established standards having little bearing on the effective use or operation of the product.

5.	Acceptable Quality Level (AQL)
The following AQL will be used unless otherwise specified.
Critical defect:	0
Major defect:	0.65
Minor defect:	1.5

6.	Inspection lots
Many practical matters influence the formation of inspection lots. The following rules should govern decisions on this matter.

6.1	Within each lot, the factors that seem likely to cause marked variability in product quality should be as nearly constant as practicable.
6.2	Inspection lots should be as large as possible, subject to the foregoing rule.

7.	Inspection results
Inspection results must be recorded in the inspection report. Refer to the section for items to be inspected.

7.1	Rejected lots must not be used in any products unless the lot has been reworked, re-inspected and found to be qualified.

7.2	The inspection report must be submitted to Atari Quality Assurance for final approval.
