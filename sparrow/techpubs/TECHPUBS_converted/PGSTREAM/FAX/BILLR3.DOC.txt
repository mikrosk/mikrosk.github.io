Dallas Office Memo


To:		Bill Rehbock
From:	Marty Pennock
Subject:	PageStream 2.2 Beta Release

Date:	June 8, 1992


I have started using PageStream 2.2 Beta Release 5. All previous problems that I was having with PageStream and GEM format files has been fixed. It is a very good package for Desk Top Publishing. I have also used Ventura 4.0 for Windows while PageStream was being fixed. I can truthfully say that PageStream is easier and quicker to use than Ventura. I enjoy using my TT030 very much. I took one home and have started using it there along with many of the other new releases like FSMGDOS and HDX 5.0. In fact, my IBM sits quite a bit of the time. I just thought you would like to hear that my problems have been resolved. I have also enclosed a couple of example pages for your viewing pleasure. I eagerly await the retail release of version 2.2.

Sincerely


Marty Pennock
