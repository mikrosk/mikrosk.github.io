SECTION ONE

INTRODUCTION


1.1 OVERVIEW


The Atari® TT030™ is designed as an integrated unit with processor, memory, and I/O
control in one package. The TT030 provides 2 Mbytes (2,097,142 bytes) of RAM memory and
is expandable to 26 Mbytes (27,262,846 bytes) by installing daughter boards. The TT030 also
provides 512 Kbytes (524,288 bytes) of ROM space. A 720 Kbyte or 1.44 Mbyte (formatted)
floppy, power supply, and floating point coprocessor are also included in the system. An
optional hard disk drive can also be added.


1.2 MAIN COMPONENTS


	•	Main Board
	•	Separate Keyboard Assembly
	•	Power Supply
	•	Floppy Disk Drive
	•	Plastic Case (upper and lower)
	•	Mouse


1.3 CASE DESIGN


The front of the TT030 contains the floppy disk drive with an eject button and busy LED.
An optional hard disk can also be installed and it also contains its own busy LED.
