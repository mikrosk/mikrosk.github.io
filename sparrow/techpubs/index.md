## Atari Microsystems Technical Publications archive

The files of Marty Pennock, technical publications, Atari Microsystems, Dallas, Texas, Oct 1991 - Oct 1992:
weekly status reports, memos, faxes, specifications, diagnostics source code and drawings.

Everything here is a conversion of [TECHPUBS.zip](TECHPUBS.zip), whose formats (PageStream 2, WordPerfect 4.1 and 5.1,
Microsoft Write, 1st Word Plus, GEM Metafile, DynaCADD, DXF, CorelDRAW 2, AutoSketch, HP-GL) no longer open in anything
current. The decoders are in [claude_harness.tar.gz](claude_harness.tar.gz).

Documents are plain text, drawings are SVG. *Pages* are the pages of a document that contain figures, rendered whole.
Where a drawing has its lettering as vector strokes instead of text, its text file notes that and lists the labels read
from the image. Duplicates of the same document or drawing were dropped, keeping the cleanest copy of each.
